HDS Drinkware at a Glance

HDS Drinkware is the public-facing custom drinkware business operated by Shanxi Huandingsheng Industry and Trade Co., Ltd., the company's approved English business name and translation. The verified Chinese legal company is 山西寰鼎盛工贸有限公司. HDS is based in Taiyuan, Shanxi, China and works as a B2B custom drinkware supplier and OEM/ODM partner.

What HDS Does

HDS helps buyers define products, compare sourcing routes, coordinate samples, confirm decoration and packaging, follow production, agree a project-specific quality-control scope and prepare international shipping. Final feasibility, price, MOQ and timing are confirmed for the exact quoted project.

How Our Supply Chain Works

HDS works with long-term manufacturing partners in Yongkang and Jinhua, Zhejiang. The partner route is selected according to product, material, component, finish, decoration, quantity and packaging requirements. HDS does not claim ownership of every factory or production line shown.

Who We Work With

Typical project types include startup brands, e-commerce sellers, distributors, corporate-gift buyers and promotional-product buyers. These are examples of buyer types, not a claim about customer counts or completed outcomes.

Customization Capabilities

Depending on the product, material, artwork and order requirements, projects may use laser engraving, screen printing, UV or full-wrap UV printing, heat transfer, labels, custom color, inserts and packaging customization. No single method is presented as available for every SKU.

Quality and Project Control

HDS coordinates approval gates for specifications, artwork, color, sample and packaging before production. During production and before shipment, the agreed scope may cover appearance, decoration, assembly, accessories, quantity, packaging and shipment preparation. The exact inspection scope is confirmed by product and project.

Shipping Support

Project-dependent shipping terms may include EXW, FOB, DDP, DDU or DAP. FOB discussions may use Ningbo, Shanghai, Shenzhen or Guangzhou where appropriate. DDP availability depends on destination and project; final responsibilities are stated in the quotation.
