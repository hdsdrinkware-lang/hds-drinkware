Scenario Requirements and Risks

In this scenario, the buyer is planning 500 insulated travel mugs for a partner summit in Munich. The proposed scope includes tissue paper, greeting cards and gift packaging, with a required event date and product-specific food-contact documentation to review before approval.

Illustrative Planning Approach

  • Confirm the exact product and customization scope: Laser engraving of corporate logo, custom individual kraft gift box with custom tissue paper wrap, and custom greeting insert cards.
  • Approve the proposed logo method on the selected surface: Laser Engraving
  • Review the complete packed sample or packaging proof: Custom matte-black kraft gift boxes with white logo print, custom cardboard inner cushion, custom printed greeting card, and individual tissue paper wrapping.
  • Write the production target only after sample approval: 15 days from sample approval
  • Compare the named shipping scope and destination: DDP Air Freight directly to corporate office in Munich

Scenario Outcome and Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance would require the buyer to approve the exact sample or signed specification, artwork, packaging proof, inspection checklist, carton data, named destination and shipping term.
