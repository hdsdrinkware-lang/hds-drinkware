Scenario Requirements and Risks

In this scenario, an Australian distributor is evaluating a mixed 2,500-piece batch of growlers and pint cups for delivery to Sydney and Melbourne. The buyer needs a written DDP scope that identifies freight, port charges, customs clearance, duties or taxes, destination handling and final-mile delivery responsibilities before comparing the landed quotation.

Illustrative Planning Approach

  • Confirm the exact product and customization scope: Laser engraved logo, custom carton mark, mixed color allocation (matte black, olive green, and brushed steel) in each shipping carton.
  • Approve the proposed logo method on the selected surface: Laser Engraving
  • Review the complete packed sample or packaging proof: Each growler in an individual bubble wrap sleeve and standard cardboard box; custom color-coded master cartons for easy distributor sorting.
  • Write the production target only after sample approval: 25 days
  • Compare the named shipping scope and destination: DDP Sea Freight with door delivery to multiple warehouse locations

Scenario Outcome and Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance would require the buyer to approve the exact sample or signed specification, artwork, packaging proof, inspection checklist, carton data, named destination and shipping term.
