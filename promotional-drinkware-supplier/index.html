When to Use This Page

This page is about supplier qualification and order handoff. Use the promotional drinkware category page when you are still choosing between cups, tumblers, bottles and gift sets.

Define the Campaign Before Requesting Prices

Give every supplier the same brief: product reference, required quantity and color split, logo artwork, individual packaging, destination, in-hands date and acceptance requirements. This prevents an inexpensive but incomplete quotation from appearing better than a fully scoped offer.

Verify the Contracting and Production Route

Match the legal entity, quotation issuer and payment beneficiary. For the selected SKU, ask who will manufacture or supply it, which sample represents the order and which specification and inspection scope will control production.

Approve Artwork and a Physical Reference

A digital mockup confirms placement, but it does not prove coating behavior, print adhesion, color, lid fit or packed protection. Approve the appropriate stock sample, logo sample or packed sample before mass production, depending on campaign risk.

Separate Product, Decoration and Packaging Costs

Request line items for the blank product, logo setup and unit decoration, individual packaging, inserts or labels, master cartons, samples and freight. This makes supplier quotations comparable and shows which change is responsible when the budget moves.

Build the Deadline Backwards

Plan from the in-hands event date through final delivery, customs or warehouse receiving, transport, inspection, production and sample approval. The factory-ready date is not the customer delivery date, and the buffer should be written before the order starts.

Confirm Carton Data Before Freight Approval

Carton quantity, dimensions, gross weight, destination and Incoterm should be confirmed before comparing landed cost. For event and promotional orders, also confirm label placement, carton marks, inner protection and any split-delivery plan.

Recommended Approval Sequence

  1. Approve the written product and order specification.
  2. Approve artwork placement and the applicable physical sample.
  3. Approve individual packaging, labels and carton marks.
  4. Freeze the inspection checklist and defect acceptance rules.
  5. Confirm final carton data and the named delivery scope.
  6. Authorize bulk production only after the earlier gates are recorded.

Comparable RFQ checklist

Use the same scope for every supplier quotation.

A comparable RFQ makes price, risk and delivery differences visible before the campaign order is awarded.

  • Exact product reference, quantity per color and destination market.
  • Logo method, size, position, artwork format and proof requirement.
  • Individual packaging, insert, label, barcode and carton-mark scope.
  • Sample type, inspection criteria and approval responsibilities.
  • Destination, in-hands date, Incoterm and freight inclusions.

How should a promotional agency compare drinkware supplier quotes?

Compare the same product specification, quantity per color, logo size and method, individual packaging, carton quantity, inspection scope, Incoterm and named destination. A lower unit price is not comparable when artwork setup, packing, freight or duty is excluded.

What should be approved before promotional drinkware mass production?

Approve the exact product sample, artwork proof, logo size and position, color or finish reference, individual packaging, barcode or label placement, carton marks and written inspection checklist before authorizing bulk production.

Can promotional drinkware orders start from around 200 pieces?

Selected stock-model projects may start from around 200 pieces with practical color, logo and standard-packaging choices. Custom colors, printed retail boxes, mixed models and structural changes can require different quantities.

What deadline information should campaign buyers provide?

Provide the in-hands event date, destination, quantity, artwork-ready date and latest acceptable sample-approval date. HDS can then separate sample, production, inspection and transport time instead of treating the event date as the factory completion date.

What evidence can buyers request from a promotional drinkware supplier?

Buyers can request the proposed legal supplier or manufacturer identity, current product sample, written specification, logo proof, packing plan, inspection checklist, carton data and available product-specific documents before placing a bulk order.
