What “Recycled Stainless Steel” Should Mean in a Quote

The claim should identify a percentage and scope, not rely on a vague product name. Ask whether the declaration covers the inner wall, outer wall or total stainless-steel mass, and whether lids, straws or other components are excluded.

Evidence Checklist Before You Publish a Sustainability Claim

  • Exact product code, material construction and declared recycled-content percentage.
  • Component scope and batch or supplier evidence supporting the declaration.
  • Destination-specific food-contact reports for the exact materials and colorway where required.
  • Approved wording for product pages, packaging and marketplace listings.
  • Clear separation between recycled-content evidence and recyclability claims.

Logo, Sample and Quality Checks

Laser engraving is often selected for a durable, restrained finish; print methods may better suit colored artwork. Approve a decorated sample and record the agreed logo size, position, color, surface finish, lid fit and packaging before bulk production.

What to Send for an Accurate Quote

  • Reference image, capacity and preferred shape.
  • Target recycled-content requirement and evidence your sales channel expects.
  • Quantity, logo file, color and packaging plan.
  • Destination country, target date and preferred shipping term.

Can HDS quote recycled stainless steel tumblers in wholesale quantities?

What evidence should a buyer request?

Request the percentage, component scope, batch or supplier evidence and the destination-specific food-contact documents relevant to the quoted product.

What is the MOQ?

Selected stock-based projects can start from 200 pieces. The final MOQ depends on the product, available material route, color, logo method and packaging.

Can the tumblers carry a custom logo?

Yes, subject to the finish. Laser engraving, silk screen and UV printing are common options, and a decorated sample is recommended.
