Quality Control for Custom Drinkware Orders

HDS coordinates product-specific quality control with manufacturing partners. The exact inspection scope is confirmed by product and project rather than presented as one fixed procedure for every order.

Request Custom Drinkware QuoteSend Quote Details

Pre-production

Before bulk work, HDS coordinates specification confirmation, material confirmation where applicable, artwork approval, color confirmation, sample confirmation, packaging confirmation and inspection-scope confirmation. Product-specific documents and destination requirements should be identified before the order is finalized.

During production

The agreed follow-up scope may cover visual appearance, logo or printing position and workmanship, assembly, product consistency, accessories and packaging workmanship. When a detail requires buyer approval, HDS may coordinate photos, videos or a reference sample. The scope and frequency are set for the project.

Pre-shipment

Pre-shipment review may cover quantity, visual condition, logo or printing, packaging, carton or label information and shipment preparation. Any additional inspection or documentation requirement should be agreed before production so the correct scope can be quoted and scheduled.

What Buyers Can Request

Depending on the selected product and project, buyers can request written specification confirmation, an approved sample, logo or artwork confirmation, packaging confirmation, inspection photos and product-specific testing documents where available. Additional product or laboratory testing may be arranged depending on the selected product, market and project requirements. Availability is not guaranteed for every model or order.

Certification and Test Scope

HDS does not publish a company-wide FDA, LFGB, SGS or ISO certification claim. Product-specific testing documents may be available depending on the selected model, manufacturing partner and destination-market requirements. Buyers should match any document to the exact quoted product and scope.
