When 200 Pieces May Not Work

A new mold, exclusive lid, Pantone body color, multiple logo positions, wrap print, fully printed retail box or custom insert can introduce a separate production or packaging minimum. Keep these requirements visible in the RFQ so the supplier does not quote a stock route that cannot meet the final brief.

What can buyers customize on a 200-piece water bottle order?

Depending on the stock model, buyers can usually discuss bottle color from available inventory, one logo position, logo size and method, standard unit packaging, label or barcode needs and carton marks. New molds, custom body colors and fully printed boxes require separate minimums.

Can colors be mixed in a low-MOQ custom bottle order?

Sometimes. Mixed colors depend on current stock by color and whether the decoration setup can be shared. Buyers should state the quantity per color because a 200-piece total does not automatically mean every color split is available.

Which choices can increase the MOQ for custom water bottles?

A custom mold, Pantone body color, exclusive lid, multiple logo positions, wrap print, complex artwork, custom insert or fully printed retail box can increase the MOQ. Each choice should be confirmed separately in the quotation.
