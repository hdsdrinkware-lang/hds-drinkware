Germany market-entry checklist

German Packaging EPR and LUCID for Drinkware Sellers

Short answer: if your business is responsible for commercially placing packaged drinkware on the German market, start by registering with LUCID. If the retail, grouped or shipment packaging is subject to system participation, also contract with a system operator and report the same packaging volumes to both the operator and LUCID.

View the 6-Step ChecklistOfficial ZSVR Quick Check
Important: This is general sourcing information, not legal advice. German packaging responsibility depends on the seller, importer, contract, packaging type and distribution route. Confirm your situation with the German Central Agency Packaging Register (ZSVR) or qualified counsel.
What LUCID covers

Packaging, Not Product Certification

LUCID registration relates to packaging placed on the German market. Food-contact and product-safety obligations for the drinkware are separate checks.

Official framework

Register, Participate, Report

For packaging subject to system participation, the official workflow has three connected duties rather than registration alone.

1. Map Who Places the Packaged Goods on the German Market

Document the brand owner, seller, importer of record, marketplace, fulfilment provider and the party legally responsible when the goods cross the German border. The ZSVR notes that overseas businesses can be responsible; do not assume the factory automatically carries the seller's obligation.

2. List Every Packaging Component

Include the product box, sleeve, insert, protective bag, label, tape, filler, shipping carton and other shipment materials. Separate reusable or transport packaging where applicable, because packaging categories can carry different duties.

3. Register with LUCID Before Distribution

Registration is completed directly in the official LUCID Packaging Register and is free. Prepare company identification data, packaging categories and the brand names visible on the packaging.

4. Join a System Where Packaging Is Subject to Participation

Retail, grouped and shipment packaging that typically becomes waste with private final consumers is generally within the system-participation framework. The official guidance says shipment packaging is almost always subject to participation.

5. Report Matching Packaging Volumes

Report material type, weight and period to the system operator and file the corresponding data in LUCID. The ZSVR requires the two reports to match.

6. Keep Evidence and Recheck Changes

Retain your registration details, system agreement, volume calculations, material weights and supplier packaging specifications. Recheck when the SKU, box, fulfilment route, seller entity or market arrangement changes.

Marketplace and Fulfilment Checks

The ZSVR states that electronic marketplaces and fulfilment providers have verification duties. Sellers should expect requests for the LUCID registration number and evidence of system participation where applicable. Build these checks into market-launch timing rather than treating them as a post-listing task.

Does LUCID apply to the tumbler or its packaging?

LUCID concerns packaging placed on the German market. Separate product and food-contact requirements may apply to the drinkware.

Do overseas online sellers need to register?

Official ZSVR guidance says commercial distributors of packaged goods in Germany must register regardless of where the company is based. Confirm responsibility for the exact sales and import arrangement.

Is registration alone enough?

Not for packaging subject to system participation. The responsible company must also join a system and report packaging volumes.

Is there a small-volume exemption?

The official guidance says obligations can apply even at low volumes, so sellers should not assume an exemption.
