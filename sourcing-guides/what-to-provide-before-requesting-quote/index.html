What Information Is Needed for a Custom Drinkware Quote?

Provide: product reference, capacity, material, quantity by color, logo artwork and placement, packaging, sample requirement, destination country and postal code, preferred shipping term, and required delivery date. If a detail is undecided, label it as open instead of letting suppliers assume different specifications.

RFQ fieldWhat to sendWhy it changes the quote
1–3. ProductPhoto/link, capacity, material and lid styleIdentifies the comparable SKU and component scope.

Copy-and-Send RFQ Format

Product: [photo/link + capacity + material + lid]. Quantity: [total + units per color]. Logo: [file + size + placement + colors]. Packaging: [type + label/barcode/carton marks]. Sample: [stock/logo/packaging]. Ship to: [country + postal code + commercial/residential/warehouse]. Term: [EXW/FOB/DDU/DDP]. Required date: [date].

If the Product Is Not Final

Send the sales channel, target user, capacity range, target landed cost and reference images. HDS can compare suitable stock-based routes, but MOQ, price, timing and compliance evidence are confirmed only after the exact SKU and destination are known.

Quote comparison

How Should Buyers Compare Supplier Quotes?

Normalize the scope before comparing price. A low unit price is not comparable when logo setup, packaging, freight or duty treatment differs.

CheckMust be statedCommon comparison error
Product scopeSKU, material, capacity, lid, color and quantityComparing similar-looking but different products.
Branding scopeLogo method, size, positions, setup/tooling and color countMissing setup fees or second-side decoration.
Packaging scopeUnit pack, insert, label, barcode, carton quantity and dimensionsComparing standard box with retail-ready packaging.
Commercial scopeUnit price, sample cost, payment terms and quote validityUsing an expired quote or excluding sample/tooling cost.
Delivery scopeProduction lead time, Incoterm, destination and freight/duty inclusionsComparing EXW/FOB with DDP as if they are equal.

Before You Approve the Quote

  • Ask the supplier to attach or identify the quoted product specification.
  • Confirm whether the MOQ applies per SKU, per color or across the whole order.
  • Confirm logo setup, sample, packaging and tooling charges separately.
  • Request carton quantity, dimensions and gross weight before final freight comparison.
  • State the Incoterm and destination precisely; for DDP, ask what is included and excluded.
  • Confirm production timing starts after which event: payment, artwork approval or sample approval.

What information is needed for a custom drinkware quote?

Provide a product photo or specification, capacity, material, quantity by color, logo file and placement, packaging, sample requirement, destination, postal code, shipping term and required delivery date. These inputs let suppliers quote the same scope.

How should buyers compare custom drinkware supplier quotes?

Normalize every quote to the same product specification, quantity, logo method, packaging, Incoterm and destination. Then compare unit price, setup or tooling, sample cost, carton data, production lead time, freight, duty scope, payment terms and quote validity.

Can buyers request a DDP quote for custom drinkware?

Yes. Provide the destination country, postal code, delivery address type, quantity and packaging specification. Ask the supplier to state what the DDP amount includes, the estimated transit window and any destination charges or services that are excluded.

What changes the MOQ for custom logo drinkware?

MOQ can change with the selected SKU, material, color, logo method, custom color requirement, packaging and whether components are stock or made to order. Selected stock-based projects may start from 200 pieces, subject to quotation confirmation.

What should a complete custom drinkware quotation show?

A useful quotation identifies the exact SKU and specification, quantity, unit price, logo scope, packaging, setup or tooling, sample terms, production lead time, carton data, shipping term, destination, payment terms and quote validity.
