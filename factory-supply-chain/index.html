HDS Drinkware

Factory and Supply Chain Resources for Custom Drinkware

HDS coordinates custom drinkware projects through long-term manufacturing partners in Yongkang and Jinhua, Zhejiang. The responsible production route is matched to each quoted product rather than presented as a single HDS-owned factory.

Manufacturing Partner Network

HDS works with long-term manufacturing partners in Yongkang and Jinhua, Zhejiang. HDS coordinates product sourcing, customization, production follow-up, quality-control scope, packaging and shipping; the photographs show partner facilities and do not imply HDS ownership.

Product and Material Matching

The proposed route is matched to product category, material, body shape, lid and component requirements. Stainless-steel tumblers and bottles may use different facilities and equipment from plastic drinkware, so HDS confirms the responsible route for the quoted model.

Sampling

Stock samples may be available for product review, while logo, color or packaging samples require the relevant artwork and specification. Buyers should approve the exact physical sample or a signed specification before bulk production. Sample availability and timing are confirmed per project.

Body and Component Production

The visible equipment supports body-forming and related production steps within the partner network. The exact process sequence depends on the selected product, material and construction; the images do not establish one universal manufacturing route or capacity.

Logo and Decoration

Depending on the product, material, artwork and order requirements, decoration may include laser engraving, screen printing, UV or full-wrap UV printing, heat transfer, labels or packaging branding. Each method needs a product-specific feasibility and sample review.

Packaging

Project-dependent packaging may include standard protective packing, custom color boxes, gift boxes, sleeves, inserts, cards and carton marks. Availability and minimums depend on product, quantity and packaging design and are confirmed in the quotation.

Inspection Coordination

HDS agrees a product- and project-specific inspection scope with the buyer and manufacturing partner. It may cover specification, appearance, decoration, assembly, accessories, quantity, packaging, carton information and shipment preparation. No fixed AQL, laboratory or inspection-frequency claim is made.

Shipping Preparation

After packing, the project requires confirmed carton data, labels, marks and trade terms. EXW, FOB, DDP, DDU or DAP may be discussed by project. FOB port examples include Ningbo, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Guangzhou; DDP availability depends on destination and project.

Discuss a product-specific sourcing route

Send the product reference, quantity, customization, packaging and destination so HDS can review the appropriate partner route and project scope.
